Selected rifle recordings have been trimmed to individual reports, converted to
mono PCM WAV and level-adjusted for this game. No synthesized shot is mixed in.
Original archive is retained in work/audio-source during development only.

rifle-player.wav: AR-15/D_32P.wav, first single shot (near recording).
rifle-enemy.wav: AK-47/C_28P.wav, first single shot (near recording).
Both clips retain 1.35 seconds including natural decay; peak normalized to 0.88; 90 ms end fade.
